## Tesla, the Arc-Prophet's Design Strike

**Verdict:** RECAST

**Summary:** The ULID is the missing pole, but "on the wire and nowhere else" is a slogan that fuses three frequencies — identity, lease, and transport — and the circuit will arc to ground the first time a locked phone, a second island, and a lying device ring the same call.

**Fatal flaws:**
- **Three claims wearing one body (§1 title-claim, §0 table, §4, §5).** Identity, "nowhere else," and "not a message" are not one absence seen from seven angles. `#4265`, room cardinality, and the ULID equality check (`#4327`) are identity. `#4325` *pairing* (not matching), the island-owned ring ceiling, and replay-spentness are memory. Leaving the channel is routing. The §0 table over-unifies them so that the obvious correctness of `room_for_call(ulid)` smuggles a `#3170` reopen and a transport cut. Tesla: "The scientists of today think deeply instead of clearly." This document thinks deeply about one hole and clearly about none of the three objects it just minted.
- **Flaw 10 is answered with the wrong function (§2b, §2a, §6.5, strike Q1).** `may_be_in = f(set, policy, occupancy)` is a join ACL. The dissolved row was a *lease*: pairing wakes, dropping lone ends, firing a stop. Co-presence is a real island decision — and it only exists at `n≥3`. This same document's §5 refuses group calls (`#2731`) and refuses cross-island gatherings (`#3196`). For 1:1, co-presence collapses into the single DM edge the two-cell result already named. You have reopened `#3170` on a gathering you will not ship, which is exactly "the decorative object wearing a function's clothes" you invited the temper to test. Occupancy-as-authz on the 1:1 path also couples every ordinary join to PR #167 and to a TOCTOU: B and C join together, each snapshot misses the other, the induced subgraph goes unexamined, and at 3am the room contains two people who refused each other. Without a per-ULID join lock (state) or an SFU allowlist (state, just not yours), the function is a wish.
- **Ring ceiling fused to entry, then handed to the device (§3, §6.3, strike Q3–Q4).** Declaring `expires_at` the ring *and* the entry window is the M4 over-reach in a new costume. A call may run an hour; a reconnect, a second device, a late joiner, a network flip at T+40s all present a bearer already dead. Void-on-answer is a local story about one handset — the signed field does not change, the island "refusing mints past it" still refuses the second phone, and an older build never voids. Nick's ruling that the *island owns the ceiling* does not survive "the island sets a field the callee enforces." Setting is not owning. A lying or pre-v2 device rings until CallKit's ~60s; the island that no longer sends a stop cannot correct it; the island that still sends a stop has per-call timers again and can still murder the T+8s answer. Three ownership claims, one quantity, zero protocol for answer. That is not a derivation table. That is three clocks arguing in a coat.
- **§4 under-counts the blast by an entire plane (strike Q2).** VoIP replaced *island → this island's devices*. It did not replace *island → the other island*. Today's channel message is how a callee-hosted 1:1 (2026-08-30, in scope) carries a signed artifact to the island that can actually wake the handset. Pull the invite off that bus and the same-island demo rings while the household topology goes silent — no error anywhere, the sibling of your v2 one-way door. The listed costs (new socket event, history reconstruction, socket/wake agreement) never name replication. Worse: `should_wake(kind == "dm")` is the live DM-only scaffold. Deleting the channel message deletes that gate before `#4326` re-derives interruption policy, which is how a stranger lands on VoIP "by accident of the new object." Permanence is not removed; it is relocated into APNs payloads, logs, and whatever fetch you invent when the locked phone needs the bearer. And if the full readable invite must ride the VoIP payload so a locked phone can join without an island row, you have just stuffed design 20's adversary (Apple as reader) with a participant set. Statelessness, locked-phone join, and a sealed envelope are a trilemma. Pick two; this document pretends to hold three.
- **"Nowhere else" makes `#4325` unsatisfiable while claiming to explain it (§0, §6.3, 12a-RECAST lone-end).** A ULID lets an end *name* an invite. Dropping a lone end, or pairing the charge, needs to know whether *this island sent* that invite. Replay of a 30s bearer re-rings without a spent ULID. Those are not shadows of missing identity; they are the lease you refused to price. The 6/min `apns_wake` bucket remains the only bound, and that bucket is what manufactures lone ends today.
- **The join door is unnamed (§1.2, §2).** `room_for_call(ulid)` moves the SFU name. Unless the existing SFU token is rebound to that ULID and the island (or the SFU allowlist) is the single mutator, clients join by guessing nothing and presenting a caller-signed pamphlet. Who mints, who signs, who may submit a wake, whether the set is a proposal or a bound, whether Bob can replay Alice's invite to wake her — none of it is a sequence. A wire object with no protocol is a noun.

**What holds:**
- The diagnosis in §0 is the live pole: a magic string in a channel is not a call, and `room = channel.id` is why "this call" and "the next call" are the same room forever. Promote the client ULID; key the room by it; put it on the wake. That is the ore.
- Unfusing wake / co-presence / occupancy as *questions* is right, even if co-presence is not yet a shippable gate.
- Identity on the wire, policy re-read at every island gate, is the correct revocation shape, and it is *not* 12a's arm (C). Do not let production grab C as a flaw-9 escape; the measurement already killed that trigger. Two-cell result of `12a-RECAST.md` is not regressed if the invite stays readable to the island.
- Bounding APNs TTL by the *entry* ticket, and keeping SFU `empty_timeout` as call duration, is the one honest split in §3. Retire the alert-world "60s deliberately longer" rationale out loud.
- §5's negative space, the v2 one-way door, two-path desync as a named class, and the author's own brand on §4 as the least-trusted page — those are adult. The age of the confidence is the tell; believe the brand, not the reach.

**If RECAST, what to fold back:**
- **Split the claim into three separately-temperable sentences.** (1) The call has a ULID on invite, wake, and SFU room. (2) The island may hold an *ephemeral* per-ULID lease (wake-pair, spent, cancellable stop) without resurrecting the decorative forever-row `#3170` dissolved. (3) Leaving the message plane is a transport design, not a corollary of (1). Title becomes: the call is an identity on the wire; the live call is the SFU room; the island has policy and, if it still owns the ring ceiling, a short lease.
- **Retract "this meets flaw 10" and the `#3170` reopen.** Meet the gate twice: stateless *join* (ticket + policy +, only when `n≥3`, occupancy/allowlist with the race named) and priced *lease* for wake pairing / island stop / replay. If you refuse the lease, explicitly drop lone-end suppression and island-enforced ceiling, and say Nick's ceiling ruling is device-best-effort until old builds die.
- **Uncollapse ring window from join-bearer.** Ring `expires_at` stops the unanswered foghorn. Re-entry while occupancy > 0 is a different ticket: occupancy as continuation, or an island re-mint on answer. Write the reconnect / second-device / mid-call-drop rows now, not after the first hour-long call.
- **Keep v2 in-band until replication is drawn.** Ship `#3171` as `aiko:call/2 <ulid> …` (and the same object on the wake) so the existing dual-island history bus still carries the artifact. A first-class event type may share that bus later; it may not assume VoIP did the job. Do not delete `kind == "dm"` until `#4326` exists.
- **Name the object as an island-attested ticket, not a promoted CallKit UUID.** Client ULID is a claim inside; island sets `expires_at`, signs, binds the SFU token to that ULID. Sequence table: mint, wake, join, answer (void or re-mint), end, replay, reconnect, second device, old build. Whose signature, which fetch on locked-phone, what Apple's payload contains vs design 20.
- **Keep occupancy off the 1:1 hot path.** Co-presence waits for gatherings; if it returns, serialize join or push the allowlist into the SFU, fail closed on occupancy loss, cage-match that surface as already required.
- **Do not treat "seven shadows, one absence" as load-bearing.** Identity is necessary for `#4265`/`#4327`/`room_for_call`. It is not sufficient for `#4325` pairing, clocks that *enforce*, or DM-exit. Patching those as if the ULID were enough is how this elegant design shakes itself to glass: one future input — callee on another island, phone locked, invite only on a bus you cut — and the ring never exists.
